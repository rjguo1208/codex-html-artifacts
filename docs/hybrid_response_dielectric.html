0. Calculation in one paragraph

The paper first computes the doped-carrier RPA response of the undoped band structure under a rigid-band occupation shift. That response is fit to a damped plasmon-pole model. The pole is then coupled to the undoped polar phonons through the analytic long-range Frohlich vertex built from Born charges, dielectric tensor, masses, volume, and phonon eigendisplacements. The hybrid modes are obtained either by solving the frequency-dependent phonon Dyson equation or, equivalently, by diagonalizing an enlarged plasmon-phonon Green-function problem. The EELS-like response is finally computed from the long-range effective electron-electron interaction, not from the phonon spectral function alone.

$$ \delta\chi^0 \;\longrightarrow\; \epsilon_{\rm el}^{-1} \;\longrightarrow\; (\Omega_{q0},\omega_{q0},\gamma_{q0}) \;\longrightarrow\; \widetilde C_q \;\longrightarrow\; \widetilde\omega_{q\beta},\widetilde V_{q\alpha\beta} \;\longrightarrow\; -{\rm Im}\,\epsilon_{\rm pl-ph}^{-1}(q,\omega). $$

Important distinction. The phonon spectral function $A(q,\omega)$ and the dielectric loss $-{\rm Im}\,\epsilon_{\rm pl-ph}^{-1}$ are related by the same hybrid modes, but they weight those modes differently. Only modes with long-range charge character contribute strongly to the dielectric loss.

1. Free-Carrier RPA Response

The supplemental material starts from the doped-carrier Lindhard response, neglecting local-field effects for the long-wavelength macroscopic response:

$$ \delta\chi^0(q,\omega)= \frac{1}{N_k}\sum_{mnk} \frac{\left(\delta f_{m,k+q}-\delta f_{n,k}\right) \left|\langle u_{m,k+q}|u_{n,k}\rangle\right|^2} {\epsilon_{m,k+q}-\epsilon_{n,k}+\omega+i0^+}. $$

The Coulomb kernel is screened by the undoped high-frequency dielectric tensor:

$$ U_q=\frac{4\pi e^2}{q^2\epsilon^\infty_{\hat q}V}, \qquad \epsilon^\infty_{\hat q}=\hat q\cdot\epsilon^\infty\cdot\hat q. $$

The interacting carrier response and inverse electronic dielectric function are

$$ \epsilon_{\rm el}^{-1}(q,\omega) =1+U_q\delta\chi(q,\omega) =\left[1-U_q\delta\chi^0(q,\omega)\right]^{-1}. $$

The paper-level route evaluates the wavefunction overlap with Wannier interpolation: diagonalize $H^W(k)$ and $H^W(k+q)$ in the same Wannier basis, then take the overlap of the two eigenvector matrices. The identity-overlap approximation is acceptable only as a small-$q$ diagnostic.

2. Plasmon-Pole Compression

The RPA frequency dependence is compressed into a damped plasmon pole:

$$ \epsilon_{\rm el}^{-1}(q,\omega)-1 =U_q\delta\chi(q,\omega) =\frac{\Omega_{q0}^2}{(\omega+i\gamma_{q0})^2-\omega_{q0}^2}. $$

For GaAs, the supplemental numerical protocol uses a $200\times200\times200$ k grid, a $0$-$0.3$ eV conduction-band window around the CBM, $T=300$ K, and $\eta=3$ meV smearing for the RPA denominator.

3. Long-Range Polar Vertex

The hybridization relevant to Fig. 1 is controlled by the dipolar long-range electron-phonon vertex:

$$ g^{\rm lr}_{q\nu} = i\frac{4\pi e^2}{Vq} \sum_\kappa \frac{\hat q\cdot Z_\kappa^*\cdot e_{\kappa\nu}(q)} {\sqrt{M_\kappa}\,\epsilon^\infty_{\hat q}}. $$

The phonon eigendisplacements are normalized as

$$ \sum_{\kappa\alpha} \left[e_{\kappa\mu}^{\alpha}(q)\right]^* e_{\kappa\nu}^{\alpha}(q) =\delta_{\mu\nu}. $$

For the long-wavelength GaAs Fig. 1 energy branches, the short-range EPW electron-phonon vertex is not required. It becomes relevant for full electron self-energy and scattering-rate calculations.

4. Direct Dyson Response

The doped-carrier nonadiabatic phonon self-energy is

$$ \Pi_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega) =\left(g^{\rm lr}_{q\mu}\right)^* \delta\chi(q,\omega) g^{\rm lr}_{q\nu}. $$

With the bare phonon Green function

$$ D^0_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega) =\frac{\delta_{\mu\nu}} {(\omega+i\gamma_{q\nu})^2-\omega_{q\nu}^2}, $$

the full interacting phonon Green function is obtained from

$$ D^{-1}_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega) =\left(D^0\right)^{-1}_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega) -\Pi_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega). $$

The phonon spectral function is then

$$ A(q,\omega)=-\frac{2\omega}{\pi} \sum_{\nu=1}^{N_{\rm ph}}{\rm Im}\,D_{\nu\nu}(q,\omega). $$

5. Hybrid Dynamical Matrix

The plasmon-pole fit lets the Dyson problem be rewritten as a frequency-independent hybrid-mode problem. Define

$$ g_{q0}^{\rm lr}=\sqrt{\Omega_{q0}^2U_q}, \qquad c_{q\nu}=\frac{g^{\rm lr}_{q0}g^{\rm lr}_{q\nu}}{U_q} =\frac{\Omega_{q0}g^{\rm lr}_{q\nu}}{\sqrt{U_q}}. $$

The self-energy becomes a plasmon-mediated phonon-phonon coupling:

$$ \Pi_{\mu\nu}(q,\omega) =c_{q\mu}^*D^0_{00}(q,\omega)c_{q\nu}, \qquad D^0_{00} =\frac{1}{(\omega+i\gamma_{q0})^2-\omega_{q0}^2}. $$

For infinitesimal linewidths, the hybrid energies are found from

$$ \widetilde C_q= \begin{pmatrix} \omega_{q0}^2 & c_{q1} & c_{q2} & \cdots \\ c_{q1}^* & \omega_{q1}^2 & 0 & \cdots \\ c_{q2}^* & 0 & \omega_{q2}^2 & \cdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots \end{pmatrix}, \qquad \widetilde C_q\widetilde V_{q\beta} =\widetilde\omega_{q\beta}^{\,2}\widetilde V_{q\beta}. $$

The eigenvalues give the hybrid energies. The eigenvectors give how much plasmon and phonon character each mode carries. For finite linewidths, the supplemental material solves the corresponding damped-oscillator eigenproblem and obtains complex eigenvalues $\widetilde\lambda_{q\beta}=\widetilde\omega_{q\beta}-i\widetilde\gamma_{q\beta}$.

6. Total Inverse Dielectric Function

The total dielectric response is derived from the long-range effective electron-electron interaction:

$$ \epsilon_{\rm pl-ph}^{-1}(q,\omega) =\frac{q^2V}{4\pi e^2} W_{00}^{\rm pl-ph}(q,\omega). $$

In the finite-linewidth hybrid basis, the supplement writes

$$ W_{00}^{\rm pl-ph}(q,\omega) =U_q+\sum_{\beta=0}^{N_{\rm ph}} \frac{ \widetilde g_{q\beta}^{\rm lr,L} \left(\widetilde g_{q\beta}^{\rm lr,R}\right)^* } {\omega-\widetilde\omega_{q\beta}+i\widetilde\gamma_{q\beta}} +(\omega\rightarrow-\omega)^*. $$

For zero linewidths this reduces to the squared-frequency expression

$$ \epsilon_{\rm pl-ph}^{-1}(q,\omega) =\frac{1}{\epsilon^\infty_{\hat q}} \left[ 1+\frac{1}{U_q}\sum_{\beta=0}^{N_{\rm ph}} \frac{2\widetilde\omega_{q\beta} \left|\widetilde g^{\rm lr}_{q\beta}\right|^2} {(\omega+i0^+)^2-\widetilde\omega_{q\beta}^{\,2}} \right], $$

where the projected hybrid charge vertex is

$$ \widetilde g_{q\beta}^{\rm lr} =\sum_{\alpha=0}^{N_{\rm ph}} g_{q\alpha}^{\rm lr}\widetilde V_{q\alpha\beta}. $$

The EELS-like output is $-{\rm Im}\,\epsilon_{\rm pl-ph}^{-1}(q,\omega)$.

9. Approximations and Implementation Notes

Overlap: the paper uses Wannier-eigenvector overlaps. The identity-overlap approximation should be marked explicitly as overlap_mode=identity_delta.

Long-range only: for Fig. 1-like GaAs branch energies, the analytic polar vertex is sufficient. The short-range vertex is needed for more general electron self-energy workflows.

Temperature: the paper states that calculations are at $T=300$ K; the carrier occupations in $\delta\chi^0$ should match that temperature.

Published files: this page contains derived formulas and workflow notes only. Raw PDFs, wavefunctions, QE outputs, Wannier files, dense RPA grids, and scheduler logs remain local.
