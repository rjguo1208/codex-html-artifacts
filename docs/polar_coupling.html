Long-Range Polar Coupling

This page records the stricter Fig. 1 coupling implementation. The previous final-branch step used the LO-TO splitting directly as an equivalent polar strength. The current path parses QE Born effective charges, the high-frequency dielectric tensor, the Gamma LO eigenvector, masses, the cell volume, and the Fig. 1 q direction, then computes the polar strength directly from the SI nonanalytic dynamical matrix. The DFPT LO-TO splitting is now an independent cross-check.

Supplement Formula

The long-range polar phonon vertex is controlled by the Born effective charge projected onto the phonon eigenvector and the field direction.

gqνlr = i 4πe Vq ∑κ q^ · Zκ* · eκν Mκ q^ · ε∞ · q^

The plasmon vertex and the phonon-plasmon coupling entering the dynamical matrix are then

gq0lr = Ωq0 Uq , cqν = gq0lr gqνlr Uq

SI Projection

The local two-mode hybrid solver uses a squared-energy matrix. The default Fig. 1 polar strength is now the SI projection of the nonanalytic dynamical matrix onto the rotated LO eigenvector.

Zν,q^ = ∑κ q^ · Zκ* · eκν Mκ
Δω2 = e2 ε0 V ( q^ · ε∞ · q^ ) | Zν,q^ | 2
Spolar = ℏ2 Δω2 , RLOTO = Spolar ωLO2 - ωTO2
cqν = ωpl Sν,q^

For cubic zincblende GaAs with one polar LO mode, the direction ratio is exactly one within the current numerical precision. The absolute SI strength agrees with the QE LO-TO splitting to 0.0315%, which is consistent with the rounded Born charges, eigenvectors, and cell volume available in the text outputs.

A local bands diagnostic from the existing 8x8x8 bands output found a direct Gamma gap of only 0.2836 eV. That explains why converging the LDA calculation cannot by itself recover a material-quality optical dielectric constant. The PBE check strongly improves the dielectric value, but fixed-cell PBE still gives epsilon_inf=17.923182183, above the GaAs reference 10.89. The next production-grade route should use a band-gap-corrected electronic structure or an externally justified dielectric/Born input before making quantitative claims about branch splitting.
