Supplement Formula

The long-range polar phonon vertex is controlled by the Born effective charge projected onto the phonon eigenvector and the field direction.

gqνlr = i 4πe Vq ∑κ q^ · Zκ* · eκν Mκ q^ · ε∞ · q^

The plasmon vertex and the phonon-plasmon coupling entering the dynamical matrix are then

gq0lr = Ωq0 Uq , cqν = gq0lr gqνlr Uq

Implemented Normalization

The local two-mode hybrid solver uses a squared-energy matrix. To avoid a fragile SI-to-Rydberg-to-eV conversion in this first direct reproduction, the absolute oscillator strength is fixed by the DFPT LO-TO splitting, while the Born/eigenvector formula supplies the mode and direction dependence.

Zν,q^ = ∑κ q^ · Zκ* · eκν Mκ
Sν,q^ = ( ωLO2 - ωTO2 ) |Zν,q^|2 / q^·ε∞·q^ |Zν,x|2 / x·ε∞·x
cqν = ωpl Sν,q^

For cubic zincblende GaAs with one polar LO mode, the direction ratio is exactly one within the current numerical precision. This is why the final plotted hybrid branches are numerically unchanged from the LO-TO-strength expression, but the coupling now has explicit first-principles ingredients and diagnostics.

Normalization Note

The local QE PH dielectric tensor is a benchmark artifact with epsilon_inf≈107, not the physical GaAs value used in the RPA Coulomb kernel. In this coupling step it only affects the Born/eigenvector direction ratio; the absolute polar strength is still anchored to the local DFPT LO-TO splitting.
