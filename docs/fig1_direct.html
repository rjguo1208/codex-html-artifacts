Policy

Direct Fig. 1 reproduction means the plotted curves must come from the actual GaAs Fig. 1 data chain: Wannier/RPA electronic dielectric response, plasmon-pole fitting, first-principles long-range coupling, the paper static construction, and Raman points. The renderer refuses inputs marked as toy, surrogate, Drude, mock, analytic, parabolic, or numerical-Lindhard surrogate.
